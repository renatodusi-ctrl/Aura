# Visual Language

The cockpit should look operational and quiet:

- dense, scannable panels;
- restrained contrast;
- clear status pills;
- compact forms;
- no decorative landing page.

The first screen is the product, not marketing.

## Mission HUD

The first glance should answer three questions: what is happening, what decision matters now and which real action is available.

- `Agora` is the primary status and CTA rail.
- `Missao` shows the selected demand without repeating the same metadata in multiple rows.
- The decision card must keep one real CTA visible, even in blocked, failed or degraded states.
- Technical detail belongs in drawers, tabs and history, not in the first reading path.
